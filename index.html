Soda Pop Groove Trainer

The low clarinets' groove is a dotted eighth and sixteenth. Swing it and the sixteenth turns into a triplet. At ♩ = 120 that's 42 ms early. Learn to hear it, then learn to play it.

Which one did you hear?

You'll hear a one-bar count-off, then the rhythm twice. Pick the version you heard.

Tap it as written

After the one-bar count-off, tap the rhythm on the pad, or with any key (the space bar works well). The click keeps going the whole time.
